May dinner is CANCELLED

April 26, 2025

Photo by Jeffrey Eisen on Unsplash

Hello friends,

It pains me to have to do this, but I have made the decision to cancel tonight’s Trans Community Dinner. The weather report shows dangerous storms forming right on top of us this afternoon, with sudden downpours, strong gusts of wind and potentially up to baseball-sized hail. Even if the storms are over by the time the dinner would begin, our volunteers would have to be out driving during the time of maximum danger. Even if they were willing, I won’t ask them to take that risk.

Thank you all for understanding. I hope to see you at our next dinner on June 19th.

Best,

Christen
